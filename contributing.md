## 🎧 About

**Minecraft NSFW Mod** is an open-source, adult-themed content modification for Minecraft that adds new entities, animations, and interactive elements to the game. Designed for mature players who want to expand their Minecraft experience with adult-oriented content.

Built for the modding community and players who are **18 years or older** and are looking for a more mature Minecraft experience. The mod integrates seamlessly with Forge and Fabric mod loaders and supports multiplayer servers.

Everything runs locally on your machine. No data is collected, no external connections are made, and all content is fully client-side. The mod is designed with performance in mind, ensuring smooth gameplay even with the additional content.

## 📂 Architecture

```bash
minecraft-nsfw-mod/
│
├── 📱  src/                  → Frontend (Java · Forge/Fabric)
│   ├── entities/            → Custom NPCs, creatures
│   ├── animations/          → Rigging, keyframes
│   └── structures/          → Dungeons, buildings
│
├── 🦀  core/                 → Backend (Java · Mod Loader)
│   ├── network/             → Multiplayer sync
│   ├── registry/            → Item/block/entity registration
│   └── utils/               → Config, commands, permissions
│
├── 🧪  tests/                → Unit + integration tests
├── 📦  assets/               → Textures, models, sounds
├── ⚙️  .github/workflows/    → CI/CD (build, test, release)
└── 📖  docs/                 → Wiki, API reference, guides
```
